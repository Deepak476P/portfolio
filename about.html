ABOUT ALEKYA

Painting is how I slow time down.

I've always noticed the in-between moments — morning light through a window, the hush of a winter forest, the particular color a memory leaves behind. Painting is how I hold on to them a little longer than they actually last.

I work mostly in oil and acrylic, reaching for watercolor or mixed media when a subject asks for it. Most pieces start the same way: a feeling I can't quite name yet, followed by weeks of trying to name it with color and texture instead of words.

Every painting in the gallery began as something small — a sketch, a swatch of color, a photograph I couldn't stop thinking about.
